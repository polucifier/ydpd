What this data can and cannot support
  • Can: show where significant historical events were recorded and compare their relative human toll over time.
  • Cannot: give a complete catalogue or forecast hazard. Older events were recorded mainly where people were present and writing things down, so histograms show the record, not necessarily how often events happened.
  • A missing death, injury or missing-persons value means not recorded, not zero. Small icons may be events with unknown tolls. Where only a category code exists, the popup shows it labeled as an estimate (wording follows the usual NOAA-style convention, so verify it).
  • Events with no year are hidden by the time slider. "Incl. related events" is a Total_* value that may include secondary effects such as a tsunami triggered by an earthquake.
  • Earthquake magnitude is stored as a whole number in this service. Icons mark event or vent locations, not the area affected. Confirm the original source of the services before citing them.
